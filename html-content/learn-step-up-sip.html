Step-Up SIP: Increase 10%/Year, Multiply Wealth by 2x

Most investors start a ₹1,000 SIP and keep it the same for 20 years. That's good. But a Step-Up SIP, where you increase your contribution by just 10% every year, creates nearly 40% more wealth over 20 years — the difference of over ₹28 lakh. Here's the exact math and why your annual salary raise makes this completely painless.

What is a Step-Up SIP?

A Step-Up SIP (also called a Top-Up SIP or Escalating SIP) is a variant of a regular SIP where you automatically increase your monthly investment by a fixed percentage or amount every year. Instead of investing ₹1,000 every single month for 20 years, you invest ₹1,000 in Year 1, ₹1,100 in Year 2, ₹1,210 in Year 3, and so on — growing by 10% each year.

The logic is simple: as your income grows with annual salary increments, your investment should grow proportionally. Most people get 8–12% salary hikes every year. Committing just 10% of that raise to your Step-Up SIP is barely noticeable month-to-month but creates an enormous impact over decades.

The Numbers: Flat SIP vs Step-Up SIP Over 20 Years

Let's compare two investors, both starting with ₹1,000/month in a 12% CAGR equity fund:

Investor A — Flat SIP (₹1,000/month for 20 years):

  • Total invested: ₹2,40,000
  • Corpus at 20 years: ₹74,000 (approx ₹74 lakh at 12%)

Investor B — Step-Up SIP (₹1,000/month, 10% increase each year):

  • Total invested: ₹6,87,000 (more invested, more compounded)
  • Corpus at 20 years: approximately ₹1,02,000 (₹1.02 crore at 12%)
  • Difference: ₹28 lakh+ more wealth
The year-by-year view: In Year 1 you invest ₹1,000/month. In Year 5 it's ₹1,464/month. In Year 10 it's ₹2,358/month. In Year 20 it's ₹6,116/month. By then, your salary has likely doubled or tripled — this amount is still a smaller percentage of your income than when you started.

Why Annual Raises Make This Easy

The genius of the Step-Up SIP is that it's psychologically painless. Consider: if you earn ₹50,000/month and get a ₹5,000 hike (10%), your Step-Up SIP goes from ₹1,000 to ₹1,100/month — an extra ₹100. You barely notice the ₹100 difference in your spending. But your investment portfolio notices enormously over 20 years.

Financial advisors call this "paying yourself first proportionally." As lifestyle inflation tends to absorb your entire raise, committing a fixed percentage to investments before it hits your spending account is the most reliable way to build wealth systematically without sacrifice.

How to Set Up a Step-Up SIP on MF Bharat

Setting up a Step-Up SIP on MF Bharat is straightforward. During the SIP setup flow, after choosing your fund and monthly amount, you'll see a "Step-Up" option. Toggle it on, enter the percentage increase per year (10% is recommended), and confirm. The app will automatically adjust your bank auto-debit mandate each year and increase your SIP instalment accordingly.

Alternatively, you can set a reminder every January to manually increase your SIP amount. Log into the app, find your active SIP, tap "Modify," and change the amount. It takes 30 seconds and can be done once a year to mirror your Step-Up strategy without automation.

Step-Up SIP for Smaller Budgets

Even a ₹100/month SIP with a 10% annual step-up is vastly better than staying flat. After 20 years with 12% returns, a ₹100/month flat SIP gives ₹7.4 lakh. The same ₹100/month step-up SIP gives ₹10.2 lakh. The habit of increasing investments regularly is more important than the starting amount. Start small. Step up. Let time do the rest.
