Most mutual funds have no lock-in period — you can withdraw anytime. The only exception is ELSS (tax-saving) funds which have a 3-year lock-in, helping you save tax under Section 80C. After 3 years, you can redeem freely with no penalty.

For equity mutual funds, money is credited to your bank in 2–3 working days (T+2). For debt funds, it's usually T+1. You redeem directly from the app — no branch, no forms, no delay. Easy hai!

SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) is like an auto-debit for your investments. Fix an amount (as low as ₹100) and a date — we automatically invest it in your chosen fund every month. No market timing needed, and you benefit from rupee cost averaging over time.
