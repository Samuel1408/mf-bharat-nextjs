Nature of Services

MF Bharat acts solely as a Mutual Fund Distributor and does not provide investment advisory services. All fund information is for general informational purposes. Investors should consult their financial advisor before making investment decisions.

Commission Disclosure

As a distributor, MF Bharat receives trail commissions from Asset Management Companies (AMCs) for distributing their mutual fund schemes. These commissions are embedded in the Total Expense Ratio (TER) of the respective schemes and are disclosed in the Scheme Information Documents (SID).

Investors may consider investing in Direct Plans of mutual funds which carry a lower expense ratio compared to Regular Plans distributed through intermediaries like MF Bharat.

Risk Disclosures

Mutual Fund investments are subject to market risks. Please read all scheme-related documents carefully before investing.

  • Past performance of the sponsor, AMC, or any scheme does not indicate future performance
  • Returns from mutual fund investments are not guaranteed or assured
  • Equity-oriented schemes are subject to high market volatility and risk
